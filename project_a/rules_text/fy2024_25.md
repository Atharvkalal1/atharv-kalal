# Salaried-individual income tax, FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26)
Sources: Income-tax Act, 1961 as amended by the Finance Act 2024 (incl. July-2024 changes); Income Tax Department.
Assumption: resident individual, salary income only. Clause IDs (R-...) are used for tracing every rupee.

## Common
R-RESIDENT: Resident individual, salary income only. Age = age at end of the financial year.
R-ROUND: Slab tax, the 87A result, surcharge and cess are each rounded to the nearest rupee (0.5 rounds up).
R-288A: Section 288A: total (taxable) income is rounded to the nearest multiple of Rs 10 (a halfway 5 rounds up) before slabs, rebate and surcharge thresholds are applied. Skipped in exact-rupee mode (apply_288_rounding = false).
R-288B: Section 288B: the final total tax payable is rounded to the nearest multiple of Rs 10 (a halfway 5 rounds up). The adjustment is shown as its own trace step. Skipped in exact-rupee mode.

## New regime (s. 115BAC(1A), default regime)
R-STD-NEW: Standard deduction from salary (s. 16(ia)): Rs 75,000 or the salary, whichever is lower.
R-NEW-BASE: Taxable income = gross salary - standard deduction (floor 0). No HRA exemption, no Chapter VI-A deductions (80C, 80CCD(1B), 80D) and no s. 24(b) loss for a self-occupied house.
R-NEW-SLABS: 0-3,00,000: nil; 3,00,001-7,00,000: 5%; 7,00,001-10,00,000: 10%; 10,00,001-12,00,000: 15%; 12,00,001-15,00,000: 20%; above 15,00,000: 30%. Same for all ages.
R-REBATE-NEW: s. 87A: if taxable income <= 7,00,000, rebate = tax, limited to Rs 25,000 (so tax is nil). Marginal relief: if taxable income exceeds 7,00,000, tax payable cannot exceed (taxable income - 7,00,000).

## Old regime (opted out of s. 115BAC)
R-STD-OLD: Standard deduction: Rs 50,000 or the salary after HRA exemption, whichever is lower.
R-HRA: HRA exemption (s. 10(13A), Rule 2A) = least of (a) HRA received, (b) rent paid - 10% of 'salary' (not below 0), (c) 50% of 'salary' if the city is Delhi/Mumbai/Kolkata/Chennai, else 40%. Nil if no HRA received or no rent paid. Rule 2A defines 'salary' = Basic + Dearness Allowance that forms part of retirement benefits + commission paid as a fixed percentage of turnover achieved. Input hra_salary_base = basic_da if given (> 0), otherwise basic_salary + da_eligible + commission_turnover.
R-24B: Loss from house property, self-occupied (s. 24(b)): interest paid, capped at Rs 2,00,000, is deducted from salary income.
R-80C: Section 80C: deduction = amount, capped at Rs 1,50,000.
R-80CCD1B: Section 80CCD(1B): employee's own NPS contribution, capped at Rs 50,000 (over and above 80C).
R-80D-SELF: Section 80D, self/spouse/children premium: cap Rs 25,000, or Rs 50,000 if the taxpayer is aged 60 or more.
R-80D-PARENTS: Section 80D, parents' premium: separate cap Rs 25,000, or Rs 50,000 if any parent is aged 60 or more.
R-OLD-TAXABLE: Taxable income = gross salary - HRA exemption - standard deduction - s. 24(b) amount - 80C - 80CCD(1B) - 80D (self) - 80D (parents), floor 0.
R-OLD-SLABS (age < 60): 0-2,50,000: nil; 2,50,001-5,00,000: 5%; 5,00,001-10,00,000: 20%; above 10,00,000: 30%.
R-OLD-SLABS (age 60 to 79): 0-3,00,000: nil; 3,00,001-5,00,000: 5%; 5,00,001-10,00,000: 20%; above 10,00,000: 30%.
R-OLD-SLABS (age 80+): 0-5,00,000: nil; 5,00,001-10,00,000: 20%; above 10,00,000: 30%.
R-REBATE-OLD: s. 87A: if taxable income <= 5,00,000, rebate = tax, limited to Rs 12,500. No marginal relief.

## Surcharge and cess (Finance Act 2024, First Schedule)
R-SURCHARGE: Raw surcharge = rate x tax after rebate, where the rate applies when taxable income exceeds: Rs 50 lakh: 10%; Rs 1 crore: 15%; Rs 2 crore: 25%; Rs 5 crore: 37% (old regime only; new regime is capped at 25%).
R-MARGINAL-RELIEF: Tax + surcharge may not exceed [tax at the threshold + surcharge at the threshold's previous rate] + (taxable income - threshold). The surcharge is reduced to meet that limit: surcharge_marginal_relief = raw surcharge - net surcharge, and net surcharge = raw surcharge - surcharge_marginal_relief.
R-CESS: Health and education cess = 4% of (tax after rebate + surcharge).
R-TOTAL: Raw total tax = tax after rebate + net surcharge + cess. Final total tax = raw total tax after R-288B rounding.
