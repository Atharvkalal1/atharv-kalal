# Indian Income Tax Provisions (FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27)
Finance Act 2025 Provisions for Salaried Individuals:

## New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC)
- Standard deduction u/s 16(ia): Rs 75,000 (R-STD-NEW).
- Income Slabs (R-NEW-SLABS):
  - Up to Rs 4,00,000: Nil
  - Rs 4,00,001 to Rs 8,00,000: 5%
  - Rs 8,00,001 to Rs 12,00,000: 10%
  - Rs 12,00,001 to Rs 16,00,000: 15%
  - Rs 16,00,001 to Rs 20,00,000: 20%
  - Rs 20,00,001 to Rs 24,00,000: 25%
  - Above Rs 24,00,000: 30%
- Section 87A Rebate (R-REBATE-NEW):
  - Taxable income up to Rs 12,00,000: Full rebate up to Rs 60,000 (tax is 0).
  - Marginal relief applies if taxable income slightly exceeds Rs 12,00,000: tax after rebate cannot exceed (taxable income - 12,00,000).

## Old Tax Regime
- Standard deduction u/s 16(ia): Rs 50,000 (R-STD-OLD).
- Deductions: Section 80C (up to Rs 1.5L), 80CCD(1B) (up to Rs 50k), 80D, HRA u/s 10(13A), Home loan interest u/s 24(b).
- Slabs and Section 87A rebate (Rs 12,500 up to Rs 5L) remain unchanged.

## Common Provisions
- Surcharge rates: 10% (>50L), 15% (>1Cr), 25% (>2Cr), 37% (>5Cr Old only, capped at 25% New). Surcharge marginal relief applies at each threshold.
- Health & Education Cess: 4% on (tax + surcharge).
- Section 288A/288B: Rounding to nearest Rs 10.
